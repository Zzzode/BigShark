# Short-Stack Push-Fold

## Core Principles

- Reduce the action set to fold, call, small open at 20 BB or deeper, and
  all-in. Avoid marginal raises that cannot continue against a 3-bet.
- Cash games have no Independent Chip Model (ICM). Compare all-in equity
  directly with chip pot odds.
- Prefer weak tables. Jam strong ranges in position against players who call
  too widely.

## Stack-Depth Bands

- **10 BB or less**: use pure push-fold when first in.
  - BTN, CO, or SB: any ace, any pair, K2o+, suited kings, Q5o+, and suited
    connectors 54s+. Tighten the margins against loose callers.
  - Early position: 22+, A2o+, K8o+, QTo+, and JTs.
  - Facing an open: continue mainly with TT+ and AQ+. Continue with 22-99 only
    when the price and opener width justify it.
- **10-20 BB**: use limps or 2-2.2 BB opens selectively in position. Respond
  to a 3-bet by folding or jamming. Tighten the jam range roughly 20% from the
  sub-10-BB range.
- **20-40 BB**: open to 2.5 BB. Build strong 3-bets so AK/QQ+ can 5-bet jam.
  Avoid wasteful 3-bet-fold lines. Small-pair set mining requires implied odds
  near 15:1, which shallow stacks rarely provide.

## Postflop at 20-40 BB

- At SPR 3 or lower, prepare to commit with an overpair or top pair with a
  strong kicker.
- Semi-bluff jam strong draws when fold equity and hand equity both matter.
- Avoid probe bets that leave an unusable stack. Bet 60-80% pot with a
  commitment plan or check.
- Against an all-in, estimate the opponent range, calculate hero equity, and
  compare it directly with pot odds.

## Buy-In and Top-Up

- Buy in short only to control exposure or avoid a strong deep-stack lineup.
  Otherwise top up to 100 BB.
- Restore the primary style as soon as effective stack exceeds 40 BB.
- Wider shove ranges are mathematical decisions, not relaxed discipline.
  Every jam still requires a position, player-count, and opponent-range basis.
